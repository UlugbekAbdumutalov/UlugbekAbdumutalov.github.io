Research

Fairness in Language-Model Attribute Inference

Undergraduate Research Assistant · UDST · Mar 2026 – Present

Investigating whether language models can infer personal attributes from social media text, and whether their predictions are equally reliable across demographic groups. Using Python, PyTorch, and Hugging Face Transformers, I evaluate models including GPT-2, BERT, RoBERTa, BART, and Pythia. Our experiments examine accuracy gaps, prediction behavior, and the difference between apparent model performance and the information models actually use.
